About the Academy

Preserving the Sound of Our Heritage

Naadhya by Kaveri is a traditional Indian music and performing arts academy created with a simple purpose: to make classical learning meaningful, disciplined and inspiring for the next generation.

We believe music is more than a skill. It carries language, history, devotion, emotion and identity. Our learning environment combines traditional foundations with a warm, student-friendly approach.

Students can explore vocal music, instruments, dance, workshops and stage performance while developing confidence and appreciation for Indian culture.
